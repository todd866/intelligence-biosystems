# Changelog

The version of record is the published paper (BioSystems 260, 105704; DOI in the
README). This repository tracks ongoing development and corrections.

## 2026-06-11 — corrections

- **Reproducibility scope.** The data-availability statement says all main-text
  numerical results are reproducible from the provided code. The Information-Density
  Tradeoff figures (the effective-dimension and task-information ranges, and the
  +55%/+158% autocatalysis comparison) are illustrative estimates not produced by the
  released scripts; the reproducible set is the VAS scaling, the code-formation usage
  statistics, and Figure 1. Treat the Information-Density numbers as illustrative.
- **Code.** Removed an unused `PCA` import from two simulation scripts.
